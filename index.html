Privacy

Your child's IEP is the most personal document you own. Klere treats it that way.

Most AI tools upload your document to a server, keep it, and learn from it. Klere never stores your child's record anywhere but your iPhone, and it never trains on it.

The AI runs on Apple Intelligence, the same on-device models built into your phone. When a document is too long to read there, you can opt in to Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Either way, every summary tells you exactly where it ran.

The default

On this iPhone only

Nothing ever leaves your device. Long documents are read section by section, so connections between distant parts of the IEP can be missed.

Your choice, off by default

Allow Private Cloud Compute

For documents too long to read on your iPhone. Apple's servers analyze the whole IEP at once, then forget it.

  • Your IEP is never stored. It's erased when the analysis finishes.
  • No one at Apple, and no one at Klere, can see it.
  • It is never used to train any model.
  • Apple publishes the server code so security researchers can check these claims.

It does leave your iPhone. That's a real difference from on-device, and it's your call to make.

What Klere does

Support for the parent, not a replacement for one.

Klere doesn't argue with the school or tell you what the plan should say. It makes sure you can read what it does say, and helps you show up prepared.

The whole IEP, section by section

Every section of the document gets a plain-language summary sitting right next to the original text. Where the IEP says something worth a second look, Klere quotes the exact sentence rather than judging it. You decide what it means.

FROM THE DOCUMENT "The team recommends extended school year services." and, two paragraphs later, "ESY is not required."

Goals that hold numbers

Klere reads each goal's own measure, like "80% accuracy in 4 of 5 trials", and asks you to confirm it. Vague goals get flagged with a question you can bring to the meeting. Then you track progress against the target the plan actually set.

On track Hard to measure Data gap

Walk into the meeting prepared

Klere finds your next review date in the IEP and starts a countdown. It builds a question list from the flags it found, gathers the numbers you're bringing, and drafts a concerns letter from your own vision and logs. Written on your iPhone, reviewed by you before you send it.

Bring data, not just memories.

Log a moment in seconds, or by voice

Reading, focus, behavior, homework, social, and wins. Tap a category and jot a line, or say "Log a moment in Klere" to Siri without opening the app. Each note counts toward the goal it belongs to, so the pattern is there when you need it.
